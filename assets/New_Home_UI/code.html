01 // ECOSYSTEM GATHERING

WHERE SRI LANKA'S AI ECOSYSTEM COMES TOGETHER

AIDSL 2026 is the flagship national event of AI Driven Sri Lanka, bringing together top global and local AI experts, innovators, and policymakers for a high-impact one-day forum focused on advancing Sri Lanka's AI landscape.

Featuring global keynote sessions, panel discussions, an innovation showcase, and exclusive networking — AIDSL 2026 is Sri Lanka's premier platform for cross-sector AI collaboration between academia, industry, startups, and government.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

AIDSL 2026 is the flagship national gathering convened by AI Driven Sri Lanka and IEEE YPSL, connecting global and local machine intelligence pioneers, researchers, founders, and policy leaders for one transformative day in Colombo.
AIDSL 2026 will take place in Colombo, Sri Lanka. Exact dates and venue will be announced soon. Detailed hall directions and delegate briefings will be dispatched to confirmed ticket holders.
The event is structured for industry professionals, researchers, startup founders, institutional allocators, policymakers, and motivated students actively pursuing AI development.
Pass pricing is structured across three tiers: Student Pass, Professional Pass, and Corporate/Partner Pass. Official pricing and registration deadlines will be announced soon. Selected fellowship opportunities exist for demonstrated research excellence.
All passes cover stage access, innovation showcase access, curated catering, official delegate kits, and institutional certificates. Higher tier passes include exclusive roundtable sessions and private fellowship lounges.
Yes, dedicated student quotas and discounted passes are strictly allocated to encourage academic participation and young talent visibility.

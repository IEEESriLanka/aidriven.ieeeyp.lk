## Brand & Style

The design system projects sovereign technological prestige, intellectual gravity, and cinematic momentum for the AI Driven Sri Lanka (AIDSL) Summit 2026. Tailored for world-class machine learning researchers, institutional sovereign wealth allocators, tech founders, and geopolitical strategists, the interface departs from Silicon Valley's overused indigo/cyan neon tropes. Instead, it commits to a deep crimson-black editorial architecture reminiscent of bespoke global summit pavilions, precision Swiss horology publications, and high-frequency command dashboards.

The visual style merges **Editorial Brutalism** with **Cinematic Dark Minimalism**:
- **Geometry & Tension:** Razor-sharp borders, exact rectangular framing, and fine structural grid lines assert institutional discipline and technological absolute precision.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep-space blacks and dense mahogany tones form an impenetrable foundation, pierced only by directional radial luminescence and focused laser-crimson accents.
- **Authority over Novelty:** Zero bubbly pill radiuses or playful micro-interactions. Every line, micro-label, and typography lockup acts as an assertive statement of national and global technological leadership.

## Colors

The color architecture relies on a specialized multi-tiered dark foundation anchored in absolute pitch blacks and deep obsidian-red sheens, punctuated by precise laser coral and crimson emissive signals.

### Primary Spectrum
- **Deep Space Black (`#080102`):** Primary canvas background providing uncompromising contrast.
- **Pure Black (`#000000`):** Recessed layers, stage framing, and hard contrast backstops.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#1A0305`):** Base structural container color for cards, table tiers, and panels.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#2B0404`):** Elevated elements, active row selections, and flyouts.
- **Deep Maroon (`#3D060B`):** Framing outlines, secondary fills, and structural divider blocks.
- **Crimson Ambient (`#5E0E1B`):** Origin source for directional field radials and heavy structural accents.

### Accent & Emissive Tokens
- **Primary Accent (`#F45C63`):** High-precision tactical coral used for call-to-action surfaces, live indicators, and cursor focus.
- **Accent Strong (`#EC3A45`):** Urgent, active push-states, error bounds, and core metric highlights.
- **Blush Glow (`#FFA3A3`):** High-tier typography highlights and glowing drop targets.
- **Cream / Primary Text (`#FFCFCF`):** High-readability warm editorial white for display typography and primary copy.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved strictly for keynote titles, numerals, and maximum-contrast focal points.
- **Muted Text (`rgba(255, 207, 207, 0.62)`): De-emphasized secondary body copy, metadata, and timestamps.
- **Hairline Border (`rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.18)`): Structural dividers and card perimeters.
- **Grid Trace (`rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.07)`): Architectural background matrix lines.
- **Focal Glow (`rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.35)`): Ambient back-glow for interactive states and primary focal elements.

### Secondary Monochromatic Palette (Block B / Technical Annex)
- **Monochrome Dark Canvas:** `#0D0D0D`
- **Monochrome Frame Lines:** `#222222`
- **Monochrome Muted Data:** `#888888`
- **Monochrome Pure Highlight:** `#FFFFFF`

### Ambient Gradients
- **Key Visual Radial Gradient:** `radial-gradient(120% 90% at 60% 40%, #5E0E1B 0%, #2B0404 45%, #080102 100%)` applied across hero horizons and key stage anchors.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes bold, mechanical geometric display headings against ultra-refined, legible, modern body proportions.

### Typographic Roles & Execution
- **Display Headings (`Space Grotesk`):** Section leads, thematic banners, and summit track headings must render in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.02em` to `0.06em`). For oversized display hero scales, tighten tracking to `-0.03em` to create commanding architectural solidity.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Balanced, unembellished, and optimized for low-fatigue consumption against deep luminescent dark backgrounds. Color values must remain anchored in warm cream (`#FFCFCF`) or muted ivory (`rgba(255, 207, 207, 0.62)`), avoiding pure white for extended reading.
- **Tactical Metadata & Micro Labels (`Space Grotesk`):** All stage markers, time codes, track numbers, and speaker affiliations are set in uppercase at `10px` or `12px` with expanded letter-spacing (`0.12em` to `0.18em`) to mimic telemetry data overlays.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column coordinate grid with thin visible structural guide lines (`rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.07)`).

### Grid Structure & Adaptation
- **Desktop (1440px and above):** 12 columns, max canvas width of `1380px` centered within generous 4rem (`64px`) canvas margins. Gutters are held rigidly at 1.5rem (`24px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** Reflows to 8 columns with 2rem (`32px`) margins and 1rem (`16px`) gutters. Sidebar content snaps into modular horizontal tiers.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns with 1.25rem (`20px`) margins and 1rem (`16px`) gutters. Heavy multi-column program schedules collapse into stacked, tabbed timeline cards.

### Editorial Spacing Philosophy
- Vertical rhythm utilizes expansive negative space between major macro-sections (minimum `120px` to `160px` desktop padding) to establish cinematic pacing.
- Component layouts maintain tight, engineered internal density (`space-sm` to `space-md`), creating a stark tension between macro-spaciousness and micro-precision.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects heavy skeuomorphic drop shadows and generic rounded soft blurs. Depth is achieved via **luminous layering**, **surface tint progression**, and **hairline edge-illumination**.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **The Abyss (Ground 0):** Pure `#080102` baseline, overlaid with the atmospheric `radial-gradient(120% 90% at 60% 40%, #5E0E1B 0%, #2B0404 45%, #080102 100%)`.
2. **Structural Slabs (Level 1):** Solid `#1A0305` flat backgrounds bordered by razor-thin `1px` lines tinted in `rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.18)`.
3. **Elevated Modules & Focus Planes (Level 2):** Shift to `#2B0404` with border opacity stepping up to `rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.40)`.
4. **Hover & Active States:** Activated cards do not physically displace vertically; instead, they cast an internal crimson edge-bleed and a diffused, low-spread back-glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.35)`.
5. **HUD Overlays & Sticky Nav:** Backdrop blur set to `12px` over `#080102cc` with a singular bottom border trace of `1px solid rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is strictly **sharp and monolithic**. Roundedness is fixed at `0` (exact `0px` border-radius, with an absolute maximum allowance of `2px` for tiny interactive elements like input tags and micro-chips to eliminate sub-pixel raster distortion). 

Key visual principles:
- **Beveled & Notched Geometry:** Key promotional hero badges and primary CTAs may feature 45-degree corner chamfers (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) measuring exactly `6px` or `8px`.
- **Architectural Framing:** Content modules present hard structural edges, with card boundaries defined purely by `1px` hairlines instead of rounded containers.
- **Crosshair & Reticle Accents:** Section corners and card junctions frequently employ `+` corner marks or subtle `L`-brackets rendered in `rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.4)`.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (CTA):** Solid sharp-edged block (`border-radius: 0px`). Background: `#F45C63`; Text: `#080102` (`font-weight: 700`, uppercase, `12px`, tracking `0.1em`). On hover: background shifts to `#EC3A45`, projecting an ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.45)`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background: transparent; Border: `1px solid rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.4)`; Text: `#FFCFCF`. On hover: border color snaps to `#F45C63`, background transitions to `rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.08)`.
- **Summit Pass Action:** Chamfered corners (`4px` notch), inline mono-spaced coordinate counter on the right flank.

### Cards & Content Modules
- **Base Surface:** Background `#1A0305`, bounded by a `1px` border of `rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.18)`.
- **Hover Behavior:** Border transitions from `0.18` to `0.5` opacity crimson. Subtle internal ambient illumination activates from the bottom corner using radial gradients.
- **Keynote / Speaker Card:** Aspect ratio 3:4. Image treated with a calibrated deep-crimson duotone or muted monochrome grade, transitioning to natural tone on mouse hover. Speaker role rendered in `label-mono-xs`.

### Chips & Track Tags
- **Structure:** `0px` radius, padding: `4px 10px`. Background: `#2B0404`; Border: `1px solid rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.25)`.
- **Text:** Uppercase `10px` `Space Grotesk`, color `#FFA3A3`.
- **Live / Status Chip:** Includes a `4px` square pulsing indicator (`#EC3A45`).

### Input Fields & Registration Forms
- **Field Canvas:** Background `#000000`, border: `1px solid rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.2)`. Height: `48px`, padding `0 16px`. Text color: `#FFFFFF`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#F45C63` with zero outline offset; casts an inner ambient glow `inset 0 0 8px rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.2)`. Placeholder text set in `rgba(255, 207, 207, 0.3)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkbox:** Exact `14px` sharp square (`0px` radius). Unchecked: `1px solid rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.3)` on `#000000`. Checked: filled `#F45C63` with an obsidian `#080102` sharp check glyph.
- **Radio:** Diamond orientation (`transform: rotate(45deg)`), `12px` box with an inner glowing crimson diamond upon selection.

### Agenda & Schedule Lists
- **Data Rows:** Divided by horizontal `1px` lines in `rgba(244, 92, 99, 0.12)`.
- **Layout:** Monospaced start-time column (`Space Grotesk` uppercase, `#FFA3A3`), track badge, talk title in bold `#FFCFCF`, speaker credentials right-aligned in muted tone.
- **Interaction:** Hovering initiates a subtle crimson-maroon scanline sweep across the row surface (`rgba(94, 14, 27, 0.25)`).

### Metric & Telemetry Counters
- **Counter Numbers:** Set in `display-hero` Space Grotesk (`#FFFFFF`), paired with a leading slash or coordinate indicator (`#F45C63`). Micro-captions placed directly below in uppercase tracking.
